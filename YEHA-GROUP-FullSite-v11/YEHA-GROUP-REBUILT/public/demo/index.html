THE BASIC IDEA

How the concept works.

A simple overview of the intended sensing and control sequence.

01

Sense

Metal electrodes detect whether water reaches selected sensing levels.

02

Decide

The controller interprets the sensor state and decides whether filling is needed.

03

Control

A properly rated control interface can command the pump ON or OFF.

DESIGN GOALS

Small concept. Useful purpose.

01

Conductive sensing

Metal electrodes are used as the water-level sensing method.

02

Compact controller

A small PCB concept intended to fit inside a protective enclosure.

03

Automatic operation

Designed around automatic pump ON/OFF control logic.

04

Clear feedback

Visual indicators can communicate the controller state.
